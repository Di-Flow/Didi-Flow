Didi-Flow privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Didi-Flow is a personal task board. It is built so that your information stays with you.

What we collect

Nothing. Didi-Flow does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your tasks are stored

Everything you enter (columns, tasks, notes, tags and dates) is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you choose to save or share one. Where you send or keep that file is entirely up to you.

Internet use

The app only uses the internet to download itself and its updates. Once installed, it works fully offline.

Children

Didi-Flow collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support contact shown on Didi-Flow's store listing.
