Backup & restore

Your board is saved automatically in this browser (with a second copy kept as a safety net). That copy disappears if you clear the browser's data, so keep a backup file somewhere safe.

Sharing straight to apps (Drive, WeChat…) isn't available here. It works when Didi-Flow is opened directly in Chrome on Android or Chrome/Edge on Windows.

Columns & settings

Columns

Tap the dot to change colour

Locking only works when Didi-Flow runs as an installed app. In a normal browser tab it follows the phone.

Help

Moving cards

Drag a card to another column. On a touch screen, long-press it first, and drag to the screen edge to reach the next column. You can also open a card and change its Column.

Checklists

In a card's notes, lines starting with [ ] become checkboxes you can tick right on the board, and lines starting with - become bullet points.

Your data

Your board is saved only on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there's no account. To keep a copy or move your board to another device, use Backup & restore in the menu.

Opened from a file

You're running Didi-Flow straight from an HTML file. The board is tied to this browser and this file location: moving or renaming the file, or opening it in another browser, shows a separate board. Restore from a backup if that happens.

Works offline

No internet connection is needed once Didi-Flow is open or installed.
